Every 50 minutes, your Mac shows you this.

Breather is a menu bar app. It covers every screen for a 5‑minute break, then gives the screen back.

Get Breather on GitHub How it works

One kind of break

50 minutes of work, 5 minutes of break. No short and long breaks, no Pomodoro, no complexity.

Bad moment? Press Esc

Esc hides the break and brings it back in 5 minutes. Skip break starts a new 50 minutes.

Time away counts as a break

Away from your Mac for 5 minutes or longer? Breather counts that as your break.

A ring in the menu bar

The icon is a ring that empties as you work. When it is empty, the break starts. Its menu pauses Breather for an hour or until tomorrow.

Install

There is no download. Tell your coding agent to build it.

Build and install Breather from https://github.com/romario333/breather. Follow the README.
